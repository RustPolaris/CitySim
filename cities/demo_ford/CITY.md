# Ford of Valdemar

## Initial conditions
- Technology: Late medieval: charcoal, water mills, hand tools, no electricity or steam
- Geography: A river-ford town in a fertile temperate valley with forested hills and an iron-bearing ridge
- History: Founded generations ago at a river crossing; grew around a market and the nearby ironworks
- Climate and resources: Temperate, reliable rain; one iron ore deposit of finite size; renewable woodland
- Initial population: 12,000; labour-force fraction 62%; unemployed share 6%

## Population requirements
- Each pop needs one unit of food per month. Food is not stocked: each month's production is all there is.
- Births 1% per year, natural deaths 0.15% per year (both on the opening monthly population).
- Starvation: starving pops = max(0, population - food). Deaths = minimum of the last three monthly starvation figures; the record resets when food covers the population.
- Collapse = population below one pop. The run also stops after 1,000 years (no collapse).

## Assumptions and abstractions
- Demo city written by hand for testing; not generated by the conceptual model.
- Demo seed economy: coefficients are per worker-month at full capacity.
- Food and services cannot be stored; everything else wears or spoils at the stated rates.
- Labour shares are fixed over time.
- Prototype limitation: no social actors, institutions, ideology or political organization exist in this model; the economy runs in isolation, so any analysis must not attribute outcomes to such mechanisms.
